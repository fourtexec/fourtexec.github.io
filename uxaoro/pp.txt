UXAORO — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: September 24, 2026
Last updated:   October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Uxaoro collects, why we
collect it, how it is processed and shared, how long we keep it, and what
rights and choices you have. It applies to the Uxaoro social network
(v0.1.0 Beta and subsequent releases) operated by fourtexec ("Uxaoro", "we",
"us"): the services at uxaoro.fourtexec.site, api.uxaoro.fourtexec.site, and
fourtexec.site/uxaoro, the web client, the standalone Beta preregistration
portal, the Android app, the desktop apps (Windows, Linux, macOS), the
Uxaoro Telegram bot, and the "Login with Uxaoro" developer platform
(together, the "Service"). Terms defined in our Terms of Use
(https://uxaoro.fourtexec.site/tou.txt) have the same meaning here.

fourtexec is the controller of your personal data.
Contact: support@fourtexec.site.

Short summary:
  * We do not show third-party ads, never sell your personal data, and do
    not embed third-party advertising or behavioural tracking SDKs in our
    apps or websites.
  * Direct and group chat messages ("Wire") and their attachments are
    end-to-end encrypted on your device. We store only ciphertext and cannot
    read them.
  * You can register with a username/email/password or sign in with Google
    or GitHub OAuth 2.0. When you use Google or GitHub sign-in, we receive
    only your basic account identifier, verified email, display name/handle
    and profile picture — never your Google or GitHub password.
  * Your profile, applied badge icons, posts, stories, comments, reactions
    and uploaded public media are visible to other users according to your
    privacy settings.
  * Public posts are checked roughly once an hour by an automated AI
    moderation agent for adult (NSFW) content.
  * We share data only with the essential infrastructure/service providers
    listed in Section 5, with third-party apps you explicitly authorise via
    "Login with Uxaoro", or when required by law.


1. DATA WE COLLECT AND RECEIVE

1.1. Account & registration data
     * Username, email address, display name (initially your username), and
       Date of Birth (`dob`, in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, used to verify that you
       are at least 13 years old).
     * Password — stored solely as a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash; we never
       store or see your plain-text password.
     * Email verification state and pending 6-digit verification codes
       (stored only as cryptographic hashes with short expiration times and
       attempt counters; if you change your email during verification, the
       pending record is updated to the new address).
     * Optional two-factor authentication (TOTP) secret and status if you
       enable 2FA in Settings.
     * Beta preregistration status if you register or sign in while Beta
       Preregistration Mode is active.

1.2. Google and GitHub OAuth sign-in data
     If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with GitHub" on the
     Login or Register screen:
     * From Google (`accounts.google.com` / Google OAuth 2.0 userinfo API):
       we receive your unique Google account identifier (`sub` / stored as
       `google_id`), your Google email address and verification status, your
       display name, and your profile picture URL.
     * From GitHub (`github.com/login/oauth` / GitHub REST API `/user` and
       `/user/emails`): we receive your unique numeric GitHub user ID
       (stored as `github_id`), your GitHub username (`login`), your display
       name, your primary verified email address (or GitHub noreply address
       if your email is private), and your avatar URL.
     * How we use it: if an account with your `google_id`/`github_id` or
       verified email already exists on Uxaoro, we link the provider ID to
       that account and sign you in; otherwise we create your Uxaoro account
       using those details. We do not receive your Google or GitHub password,
       and we do not access your Google Drive, Gmail messages, or private
       GitHub repositories.

1.3. Security challenge (Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA)
     To protect login and registration from automated abuse, we use
     Cloudflare Turnstile. When you complete the challenge, our server
     verifies the Turnstile token with Cloudflare and issues a short-lived,
     single-use CAPTCHA pass stored as a SHA-256 hash bound to your client
     IP address and expiration timestamp.

1.4. Profile, appearance & privacy settings
     * Optional profile fields: bio, status quote, city (only text you type
       manually — we never request GPS or device location), avatar image,
       and cover image.
     * Uxaoro Premium customisation: profile glow, name colour, name style,
       Premium badge, chat bubble style, and profile theme.
     * Collectible Badges: badges you own (claimed via promocodes or sales)
       and whether each badge is currently set to `applied` (icon shown next
       to your name) or `unapplied` (hidden).
     * Privacy preferences: who can message you, call you, see your
       last-seen timestamp, see your online indicator, send you friend
       requests, invite you to communities, add you to group chats, and view
       your stories; plus your "Show NSFW content" and "My account is NSFW"
       toggles.

1.5. Social content and activity
     * Posts, wall posts, reposts, comments, likes, emoji reactions, Smiles
       selections, and saved/bookmarked posts (`More -> Saved posts`).
     * Media files you upload: photos (including edits/filters applied in
       the built-in photo editor), videos, audio tracks, community avatars
       and covers, and custom stickers/sticker packs (plus your favorite
       stickers list). Uploaded images are processed and re-encoded on our
       server; we do not perform facial recognition or build biometric
       identifiers.
     * Stories (automatically hidden after 24 hours) and the list of users
       who viewed each story.
     * Post view records (recorded once per user per post to credit UX view
       rewards and prevent duplicate payouts).
     * Social graph: friends, incoming and outgoing friend requests,
       communities ("Circles") you created or joined and your role in them,
       and users you have blocked.
     * Moderation reports you submit (reported item, category, details).

1.6. End-to-end encrypted messages ("Wire")
     Direct messages and group chat messages (and their media attachments)
     are encrypted on your device using a random AES-256-GCM key, which is
     wrapped with the RSA-OAEP public key of each chat participant. Our
     servers store:
     * the encrypted message body (`ciphertext`), initialization vector
       (`nonce`), encrypted attachment files, and per-recipient wrapped AES
       keys — we cannot decrypt any of these;
     * your RSA public key (your private key stays on your device);
     * unencrypted routing metadata required to operate chat features: chat
       IDs, group chat titles, ownership, member lists and roles, group
       invite link tokens (`invite_token`), sender ID, timestamp, media/
       sticker type, emoji reactions on messages, read timestamps, pinned
       and muted chat flags, and deletion status.

1.7. Audio, video & screen-share calls
     Calls are peer-to-peer WebRTC connections encrypted in transit with
     DTLS-SRTP. Our server only relays ephemeral signalling messages (invite,
     SDP offer/answer, ICE candidates, hang-up) between participants in real
     time and does not record or store call audio, video, screen-share
     streams, or signalling payloads. Because WebRTC connects peers
     directly, your IP address is shared with the other call participant and
     with the STUN/TURN servers used for NAT traversal.

1.8. UX Wallet, Uxaoro Premium, Sales, Badges & Payments
     * Your UX balance and transaction ledger (`ux_transactions`: gifts sent
       and received with optional message, CryptoBot top-ups, community-join
       and post-view rewards, promotional Sale purchases in UX, Premium
       purchases, and administrator grants/deductions).
     * Promotional Sales (`sales` & `user_sales`) and Badge Promocodes
       (`badges`, `badge_promocodes`, `user_badges`): which sales or
       promocodes you claimed, UX paid, Premium days granted, and badges
       unlocked.
     * Cryptocurrency top-ups: invoice ID, UX amount, asset (e.g. USDT),
       price, status, and timestamps. Payment processing occurs inside
       Telegram via CryptoBot (Crypto Pay); we never receive your crypto
       wallet private keys, seed phrases, or bank card numbers.
     * Telegram account link (optional): if you link the Uxaoro Telegram
       bot, we store your Telegram user ID, Telegram username, and temporary
       linking code.

1.9. In-App News (`More -> News` and Popup News)
     When your app or browser loads Uxaoro News, it fetches announcement
     configuration (`config.json`), Markdown (`news.md`), and HTML pages
     from `https://fourtexec.site/uxaoro/news/` (or via the Uxaoro server's
     `/news` proxy) and filters them locally on your device by platform
     (`android`, `desktop`, `web`) and app version (`0.1.0`). Dismissal state
     for one-time Popup News (`uxa_news_pop_<id>` / `news_pop_<id>`) is
     stored locally on your device.

1.10. Developer platform ("Login with Uxaoro")
      * Developer enrollment status, hashed email verification codes,
        developer projects (name, description, website, allowed redirect
        URIs), and hashed project API keys.
      * OAuth 2.0 authorisations (`oauth_codes` and `oauth_tokens`, stored
        hashed) for third-party apps you sign into using "Login with
        Uxaoro".

1.11. Support communications
      Emails and attachments you send to support@fourtexec.site and our
      responses.


2. DATA COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

2.1. Session tokens: short-lived JWT access tokens and hashed refresh tokens
     (with expiration and revocation state).
2.2. Presence: last-seen timestamp and online connection state (visible to
     others only as permitted by your privacy settings).
2.3. Notifications: in-app alerts generated for you (likes, comments, friend
     requests, UX gifts, etc.) and their read status.
2.4. Technical & rate-limit logs: your IP address is processed in memory to
     enforce per-endpoint rate limits (20 requests/second by default) and to
     bind single-use CAPTCHA passes. Standard web server / reverse-proxy
     logs (IP address, timestamp, HTTP method and path, status code, user
     agent) are kept for security and debugging and rotated periodically
     (typically within 30 days) unless required to investigate an attack or
     abuse.
2.5. No tracking cookies or ad SDKs: we do not use advertising IDs,
     cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics/telemetry SDKs.


3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE & APP PERMISSIONS

3.1. Local storage. The web, desktop and Android clients store on your
     device:
     * your session tokens (`uxa_access`, `uxa_refresh`);
     * your E2EE RSA private key (stored only on your device);
     * an offline cache of feeds, profiles, stickers, and decrypted messages
       for fast loading;
     * unsent message drafts, dismissed Popup News IDs, and appearance
       preferences (theme: system/light/dark, AMOLED mode, accent colour,
       text size, compact mode, reduced motion, Smiles style).
     Logging out clears your active session and local cache; uninstalling
     the app or clearing browser storage removes your E2EE private key
     unless you exported a backup in Settings.
3.2. Android permissions. The Android app requests:
     * `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` to connect to Uxaoro;
     * `CAMERA` and `RECORD_AUDIO` / `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` only when you
       initiate a voice/video call or capture media.
     The Android app does NOT request access to your contacts, GPS location,
     SMS, call logs, or package-installation (`REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES`)
     permissions.

6. AUTOMATED AI CONTENT MODERATION

Roughly once an hour, our automated moderation agent checks new public posts
that have not yet been classified. For each post, it sends to the configured
AI model endpoint: the author's username and display name, community name
(if posted in a community), post text, media type/metadata, and up to two
attached photos (read from local server storage only). The model returns a
JSON verdict indicating whether the post is NSFW, a confidence score,
categories, and a short reason, which we record on the post and in
`moderation_log`. Flagged posts are blurred/hidden from everyone who has not
enabled "Show NSFW content". If at least 50% of an account's posts within 7
days (with at least 4 posts) are flagged NSFW, the account receives an
automatic NSFW mark locked to staff review. Staff can inspect and override
any AI verdict; you may request human review at support@fourtexec.site.

Private messages in "Wire" are end-to-end encrypted and are never sent to
the AI moderator.

password hash, Google ID (`google_id`), GitHub ID (`github_id`), 2FA secret,
unapplied (hidden) Badges, saved posts, blocked users, moderation reports
you filed, UX purchase invoices, Telegram link details, and private chats
you are not a member of.


8. DATA RETENTION & ACCOUNT DELETION

 * Account and profile data (including linked `google_id` or `github_id` and
   owned Badges): kept while your account remains active.
 * Posts, comments, and messages: kept until you delete them or delete your
   account.
 * Stories: hidden from other users after 24 hours and cleaned up
   periodically or upon account deletion.
 * Pending email verifications, 2FA challenges, CAPTCHA passes, and OAuth
   authorisation codes: deleted automatically when used or expired (within
   minutes to hours).
 * UX transaction ledger and payment records: retained while your account
   exists and for the period required afterwards for accounting, fraud
   prevention, and legal compliance.
 * Account deletion: email support@fourtexec.site from the email address
   linked to your account. After verifying ownership, we will delete or
   irreversibly anonymise your account, profile, OAuth links, posts,
   comments, stories, reactions, uploaded files, and your copies of messages
   within 30 days (except for records we are legally required to retain).


9. SECURITY MEASURES

We protect your data using HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit (with native TLS
support and Android TLS proxy-interception guards), client-side AES-256-GCM
and RSA-OAEP end-to-end encryption for private messages and chat files,
PBKDF2-SHA256 password hashing, SHA-256 hashing for refresh tokens, CAPTCHA
passes, verification codes and API keys, HMAC-signed OAuth state tokens,
optional TOTP 2FA, first-frame WebSocket authentication (keeping tokens out
of URLs), and per-IP sliding-window rate limiting.


10. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK, the
CCPA/CPRA in California, and Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal
Data"), you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, or restrict
processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

Directly inside the Uxaoro app (`You` and `More -> Settings`), you can:
 * edit your display name, bio, status, city, avatar, and cover;
 * toggle any owned Badge between Applied (visible icon) and Unapplied
   (hidden);
 * configure granular privacy controls for messages, calls, stories, online
   status, last-seen time, friend requests, comunidades, and group invites;
 * toggle "Show NSFW content" and "My account is NSFW" (unless locked by
   moderation);
 * change your password, enable or disable 2FA, and export/import your E2EE
   private key;
 * delete your posts, comments, stories, and messages, block users, and
   revoke "Login with Uxaoro" app authorisations.

For data export requests, unlinking OAuth providers, or full account
deletion, contact support@fourtexec.site. We respond within 30 days.


11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Uxaoro requires all users to be at least 13 years old and verifies Date of
Birth (`dob`) at registration. We do not knowingly collect personal data
from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has accessed Uxaoro,
contact support@fourtexec.site and we will promptly terminate and delete the
account.
